CLF-MCBF for safe HCMG mode switching

Matrix-valued control barrier functions applied to hybrid control moment gyroscope mode switching, so the gimbals lock and unlock without passing through a singular configuration.

Paper in preparation

Summary

  • Applied matrix-valued control barrier functions to hybrid control moment gyroscope (HCMG) mode switching, solving a constrained semidefinite optimization for safe gimbal locking and unlocking.
  • Extending the method to a unified CLF-CBF controller that enforces stability and safety without a nominal controller.
  • Validated nonsingular gimbal locking during various maneuvers in MATLAB.
  • Currently drafting a conference paper as the first author.
