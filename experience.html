Space Systems Group (SSG)

Undergraduate researcher. Advisor: Dr. Fitz-Coy. Gainesville, Florida

Honors thesis: HCMG momentum management using magnetorquers

  • Built a MATLAB attitude and orbit simulation of a 12U CubeSat for rapid retargeting and precision nadir pointing in sun-synchronous orbit, under gravity-gradient, aerodynamic-drag, and J2 perturbations.
  • Implemented generalized singularity-robust steering for the HCMG array, using null motion to reach a nonsingular gimbal configuration before locking into reaction-wheel (RW) mode.
  • Applied cross-product magnetorquer desaturation, limiting RW speed growth over 15 simulated orbits; without desaturation, speeds drifted within three orbits, leading to loss of control authority.
  • Reviewed passive and active desaturation methods, HCMG steering laws, and spacecraft disturbance models.

HARMONIA: unified docking and debris-capture architecture

Completed November 11, 2026
