  1. Space Systems Group (SSG)

    Undergraduate researcher. Advisor: Dr. Fitz-Coy. Gainesville, Florida

    Spring 2026 to present Research

    Honors thesis: HCMG momentum management using magnetorquers

    • Built a MATLAB attitude and orbit simulation of a 12U CubeSat for rapid retargeting and precision nadir pointing in sun-synchronous orbit, under gravity-gradient, aerodynamic-drag, and J2 perturbations.
    • Implemented generalized singularity-robust steering for the HCMG array, using null motion to reach a nonsingular gimbal configuration before locking into reaction-wheel (RW) mode.
    • Applied cross-product magnetorquer desaturation, limiting RW speed growth over 15 simulated orbits; without desaturation, speeds drifted within three orbits, leading to loss of control authority.
    • Reviewed passive and active desaturation methods, HCMG steering laws, and spacecraft disturbance models.

    HARMONIA: unified docking and debris-capture architecture

    Fall 2026. Completed November 11, 2026

    • Retrieved folded Mini-MegaTORTORA light curves using Python and determined rotation rates for 44 defunct low-Earth-orbit satellites, resolving factor-of-two period ambiguities.
    • Estimated grapple radii, the distances from spin axes to capture points, using satellite bus and adapter-ring dimensions, and analyzed trends across the dataset.
    • Analyzed rotation rate versus time since end of life across the dataset, identifying a trend toward lower rates among satellites inactive for longer.
    • Generated Hohmann-phasing and Clohessy–Wiltshire relative-motion plots for far-field rendezvous and proximity operations supporting the lead researcher's PhD dissertation.

  4. Lunar hopper trajectory optimization and navigation

    Senior design project. Astrodynamics and guidance, navigation lead. Completed November 11, 2026

    Fall 2026 Engineering project

    • Built a minimum-wet-mass outbound-and-return lunar hop optimizer in Python using Hermite–Simpson collocation and IPOPT, enforcing terrain, landing, and sensor-pointing constraints with NASA south-pole elevation data.
